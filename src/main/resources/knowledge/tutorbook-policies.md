# TutorBook Policies and FAQ

Knowledge source for TutorBook's grounded Q&A (Milestone 4, RAG). This is a
small hand-written dataset for the demo scenario: a campus tutoring center at
SJSU. Each "## " section is embedded as one chunk.

## Opening hours
The TutorBook tutoring center is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
and Saturday from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM. It is closed on Sundays and on SJSU
campus holidays. Tutors publish their own available slots inside these hours.

## Location and online sessions
In-person sessions take place in the Student Success Center, MLK Library room 2B.
Online sessions use the Zoom link that the tutor sends in the confirmation; the
student should join from a quiet place with a working microphone.

## How to book a session
Students book an open slot on the Available Slots page or through the TutorBook
assistant. A slot can be booked by only one student. Bookings close when the
session starts. Every booking gets a confirmation notification with the
appointment number.

## Booking limits
A student can hold at most 3 upcoming bookings at a time and at most one
booking per tutor per day, so that every student gets a fair chance at popular
time slots.

## Cancellation policy
Students can cancel a booking for free until the session starts, on the My
Appointments page or through the assistant. Please cancel at least 12 hours
before the session so another student can take the slot. Cancelled slots
become available to other students again immediately.

## Late arrival and no-show policy
Tutors wait 10 minutes. A student who is more than 10 minutes late may be
marked as a no-show and the session is not extended. After 2 no-shows in one
semester, a student cannot book for 2 weeks.

## Prices and payment
Session prices are shown with each service, for example Calculus Tutoring is
$40.00 for 60 minutes. Payment is collected at the session; TutorBook does not
store card details. SJSU students with an EOP or Promise Scholars waiver can ask
the front desk to waive the fee.

## Services offered
TutorBook offers Calculus Tutoring (60 min), Java Programming Help (60 min),
Physics Problem Session (45 min), Essay Review (30 min) and the Exam Prep Crash
Course (90 min). Each service is taught by tutors who specialize in it.

## What to bring
Bring your SJSU ID, your course syllabus or assignment, and the specific
problems or questions you want to work on. For programming help, bring a laptop
with your code and the exact error message. For essay review, bring a draft
that is at least partly written.

## Academic integrity
Tutors explain concepts and help you practice, but they do not complete graded
homework, take-home exams or projects for you. Requests to do so end the
session. Follow your instructor's collaboration policy.

## Bringing friends and group sessions
Sessions are one-on-one by default. Up to 2 classmates from the same course can
join if the booking student adds a note when booking; the price does not change.

## Reminders and notifications
TutorBook sends a confirmation when you book, a notice when you cancel, and a
personalized reminder about 24 hours before each session.

## Accessibility
Students with accommodations through SJSU's Accessible Education Center (AEC)
can request extra time or an accessible room by adding a note to the booking or
emailing the front desk.

## Contact
Questions that are not answered here: email the front desk at
tutorbook@sjsu.edu or visit MLK Library room 2B during opening hours.
